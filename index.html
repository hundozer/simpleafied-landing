01 — Products

Three products,
for three kinds of work.

VERITAS

Quality management

For QA teams at biotech companies, CROs and GMP manufacturers of 10–100 people.

  • Controlled documents with review, approval and release
  • Training with read-and-understood signatures
  • Change control, deviations and CAPA
  • Equipment calibration and QP certification

GENESIS

Cultivation and traceability

For licensed growers who need to show where every lot came from.

  • Mother plants, clones, flowering and drying
  • Plant batches and harvest lots

  • Destruction, recalls and lot release

NEXUS

Laboratory and LIMS

  • Online test orders and sample tracking

  • Certificates anyone can check by QR code

02 — How it connects

Use them together and you can see
whether a batch is ready to ship.

  1. GENESISGrownYour growers record the plants, rooms and harvest lot
  2. NEXUSTestedThe lab adds its results and certificate for that lot
  3. VERITASCheckedQA reviews open deviations, CAPAs and changes
  4. LIMITSComparedYou see each result next to the limits of the market you ship to
  5. RELEASEReleasedYour QP sees what is open, decides and signs

We are building this release view with our first pilot customers. We are starting with medical-cannabis producers who supply Germany.

03 — How we work

We build for people who
answer to an inspector.

01

Your people make the decisions

We point out what looks wrong and explain why. Your qualified people approve, release and sign.

02

You can always see why

Next to every check you see the rule, the limit and the record it came from. We don’t show scores we can’t explain.

03

We record who did what

You sign in again each time you sign electronically. Nobody can edit the audit trail.

04

We design around the rules

We design around EU GMP Annex 11 and 21 CFR Part 11. You validate it for your own use, and we give you documents to help.
